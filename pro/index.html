Introducing SignShield+

Peace of mind for the signs you trust us to manage.

Your signs work hard. They're exposed to wind, weather, installation, transportation, and everything else that comes with life in the field. SignShield+ adds another layer of protection to qualifying sign inventory managed by Pro Real Estate Signs.

Discover SignShield+

Things Happen

Sometimes a sign comes back ready for the next listing. Sometimes it doesn't.

When qualifying physical damage leaves an eligible sign beyond reasonable repair, SignShield+ helps put a fresh replacement back into your inventory without another unexpected full-price replacement expense.

Wind Damage

Severe bending, impact damage, and other irreparable damage caused while your sign is in the field.

Torn Grommets

When the grommet and surrounding panel material are torn or damaged beyond reasonable repair.

Smashed Corners

Crushed or severely damaged corners that leave the panel unsuitable for continued professional use.

Permanent Graffiti

Vandalism or permanent markings that cannot reasonably be cleaned or restored.

How SignShield+ Works

We already manage your signs. Now we can help protect them, too.

Because your inventory already passes through our hands, the process stays simple.

Damage Happens

An eligible inventoried sign suffers qualifying physical damage after enrollment.

We Inspect It

If it can reasonably be cleaned or repaired, we keep it in service. If the damage is beyond reasonable repair, it may qualify for replacement.

We Replace It

Once approved, a fresh replacement is produced and returned to your managed inventory.

Peace of Mind

One less thing to worry about.

SignShield+ isn't a sign refresh program. It's protection for the unexpected physical damage that happens when signs spend their lives in the field. If a qualifying sign is damaged beyond reasonable repair, we'll take it from there.
