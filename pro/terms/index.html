SignShield+

Membership Terms

These terms explain eligibility, replacement benefits, exclusions, artwork requirements, and membership obligations for Agent SignShield+.

1. Membership Term

Agent SignShield+ is a 12-month membership program. Membership begins on the date payment is successfully completed and remains active for 12 months, subject to these terms.

2. Eligible Inventory

SignShield+ applies only to qualifying signs and riders that are currently inventoried and managed by Pro Real Estate Signs.

Eligible primary sign panels may be up to 24 inches by 30 inches.

Eligible original materials may include ACM, aluminum, PVC, 10mm coroplast, and other approved rigid sign substrates.

Original signs manufactured from 3mm or 4mm coroplast are not eligible.

3. Replacement Allowance

Each Agent SignShield+ membership includes up to 6 qualifying replacements per membership year.

Each physical sign panel or rider replaced counts as one replacement toward the annual allowance.

Unused replacements do not roll over, transfer, or have cash value.

4. Qualifying Physical Damage

Replacement benefits apply only to qualifying physical damage that occurs after the membership effective date and leaves the eligible sign unsuitable for continued professional use.

Examples of qualifying damage may include:

  • Irreparable wind damage
  • Torn-out grommets or damaged surrounding panel material
  • Smashed or severely bent corners
  • Punctures, cracks, or severe impact damage
  • Permanent graffiti or vandalism that cannot reasonably be removed
  • Other physical damage determined by Pro Real Estate Signs to be beyond reasonable repair

5. Repair Before Replacement

SignShield+ is not a routine sign replacement or refresh program.

If an item can reasonably be cleaned, repaired, restored, or returned to professional service, replacement benefits do not apply.

Pro Real Estate Signs has final authority to determine whether an item can reasonably be repaired or qualifies for replacement.

6. Exclusions

SignShield+ does not provide replacement benefits for:

  • Missing signs or riders
  • Stolen signs or riders
  • Pre-existing damage
  • Normal wear and tear
  • Minor cosmetic imperfections
  • Fading or discoloration from age or exposure
  • Outdated branding, contact information, or artwork
  • Voluntary replacement of otherwise serviceable signs
  • 3mm or 4mm coroplast signs
  • Signs not currently inventoried and managed by Pro Real Estate Signs
  • Damage that can reasonably be cleaned, repaired, or restored

7. Damaged Item Must Be Present

The damaged sign or rider must be physically recovered and surrendered to Pro Real Estate Signs before a replacement may be approved.

SignShield+ cannot be used to increase a customer's sign inventory.

8. Artwork Requirements

Replacement benefits cover reproduction of the qualifying damaged physical sign only.

The customer must provide production-ready artwork or have suitable production-ready artwork already on file with Pro Real Estate Signs.

If new artwork, design work, artwork reconstruction, or artwork preparation is required, the customer is responsible for the applicable design fee. Design services are billed at $29.99 per hour with a one-hour minimum.

Changes to branding, layout, photography, logos, contact information, or other artwork are not included as part of the replacement benefit.

9. Replacement Material

Approved replacements will generally be produced on ACM unless Pro Real Estate Signs determines another material is appropriate.

10. Membership Payments

Annual memberships are paid in full at enrollment.

Monthly memberships require a 12-month commitment and are billed monthly according to the selected payment plan.

Membership benefits require the account to remain active and payments current.

11. Missed or Failed Payments

If a required membership payment fails or becomes past due, SignShield+ benefits may be suspended until the account is brought current.

Suspension of benefits does not automatically cancel or shorten the 12-month membership commitment.

12. Approval of Replacements

All replacement requests are subject to inspection, verification, eligibility review, and approval by Pro Real Estate Signs.

The customer does not have authority to independently approve a SignShield+ replacement.

13. Program Changes

Pro Real Estate Signs may modify SignShield+ pricing, eligibility requirements, materials, benefits, or terms for future membership terms.

Changes will not reduce replacement benefits already purchased for an active membership term unless required by law or mutually agreed upon.

14. No Cash Value

SignShield+ replacement benefits have no cash value and cannot be exchanged for credits, refunds, unrelated products, printing services, installation services, or other services.

15. Membership Acceptance

By purchasing Agent SignShield+, the customer acknowledges that they have reviewed and accepted these membership terms.

Questions?

Contact Pro Real Estate Signs before enrolling if you have questions regarding eligible inventory, qualifying damage, artwork requirements, or any other SignShield+ membership term.
