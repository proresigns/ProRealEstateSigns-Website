What's Protected

Built for real physical damage.

SignShield+ is designed for qualifying physical damage that makes a managed sign unsuitable for continued professional use.

Examples That May Qualify

  • Irreparable wind damage
  • Torn-out grommets and surrounding material
  • Smashed or severely damaged corners
  • Permanent graffiti that cannot be cleaned
  • Other qualifying physical damage beyond reasonable repair

What's Not Included

  • Missing or stolen signs
  • Pre-existing damage
  • Normal wear and aging
  • Fading or outdated artwork
  • Damage that can reasonably be cleaned or repaired
  • 3mm and 4mm coroplast

Artwork Requirements

Replacement production requires production-ready artwork supplied by the customer or artwork already on file with Pro Real Estate Signs. If new artwork or design work is required, normal design fees apply.
