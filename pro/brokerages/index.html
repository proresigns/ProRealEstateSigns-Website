Built for Larger Inventories

Protect the fleet without turning sign damage into another operational problem.

Brokerage inventories can include dozens or hundreds of signs moving through installations, removals, transportation, storage, weather, and repeated field use.

Brokerage SignShield+ is designed to add a layer of peace of mind around that managed inventory while keeping replacement decisions centralized and consistent.

Brokerage SignShield+ may include:

  • Coverage structured around qualifying managed inventory
  • Replacement benefits for qualifying irreparable physical damage
  • Centralized inspection and approval by Pro Real Estate Signs
  • Fleet-level administration
  • Support for qualifying brokerage-owned panels and riders
  • Custom coverage options based on inventory size

How It Works

Simple protection built into professional sign management.

Because your qualifying brokerage inventory is already managed by Pro Real Estate Signs, the process stays simple.

Damage Happens

A qualifying brokerage-owned sign sustains physical damage after enrollment.

We Inspect It

If the sign can reasonably be cleaned, repaired, or restored, it stays in service. If not, it may qualify for replacement.

We Handle the Replacement

Approved replacements are produced and returned to your managed brokerage inventory.
