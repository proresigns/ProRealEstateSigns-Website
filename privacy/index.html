Pro Real Estate Signs

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Pro Real Estate Signs, LLC collects, uses, and protects information provided through our website, payment systems, and services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us when requesting an account, placing an order, contacting us, enrolling in a program, making a payment, or otherwise using our services.

This information may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business or brokerage name
  • Billing information
  • Property or service addresses
  • Order and service information
  • Sign inventory and related account information
  • Communications you send to us

2. Payment Information

Payments may be processed through third-party payment processors, including Stripe. Payment information submitted through a third-party payment processor is handled according to that provider's privacy and security practices.

Pro Real Estate Signs does not directly store complete payment card numbers submitted through Stripe-hosted checkout.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Provide and manage our services
  • Process orders and payments
  • Manage customer accounts and sign inventory
  • Schedule installations, removals, and service work
  • Provide customer support
  • Communicate regarding orders, accounts, and services
  • Administer memberships and programs such as SignShield+
  • Improve our website, services, and business operations
  • Maintain records and prevent fraud or misuse
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

4. Website Analytics

Our website may use analytics technologies, including Google Analytics, to help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

These technologies may collect information such as browser type, device type, pages visited, referring pages, approximate geographic information, and other website usage data.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and service providers may use cookies and similar technologies to operate website features, remember preferences, measure website performance, analyze traffic, and support security.

Browser settings may allow you to limit or disable certain cookies. Doing so may affect some website functionality.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate our business and provide services. These providers may include payment processors, technology providers, hosting providers, analytics providers, communications services, and other vendors supporting our operations.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property, security, or operations of Pro Real Estate Signs, our customers, or others.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and operational measures intended to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and satisfy legal, accounting, or regulatory obligations.

9. Third-Party Services

Our website and services may contain links to or integrate with third-party websites and services. Pro Real Estate Signs is not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party services they use.

10. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and real estate professionals and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, technology, business practices, or legal requirements.

Updates will be posted on this page with the revised effective date.

12. Contact Us

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information may be directed to:

Pro Real Estate Signs, LLC
